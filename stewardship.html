Your Digital Boat Care Journal

Your boat's history. What it needs now. What comes next.

Your Journal keeps your vessel's condition, care history, photos, completed work and upcoming maintenance together in one place.

We track upcoming needs and remind you when it's time to take action — so you don't have to manage the maintenance calendar yourself.

The Journal belongs with your vessel and remains yours even if you stop using Vessel Steward.

What your membership includes

Two visits. Every month.

Each month we visit your vessel twice to check its condition inside and out, perform basic system checks, document changes and rinse exterior surfaces with fresh water.

Regular freshwater rinsing removes accumulated salt, dust and surface contamination, helping prevent buildup between deeper maintenance.

  • Deck and exterior surfaces
  • Wood and teak
  • Stainless and hardware
  • Windows, hatches and seals
  • Visible water intrusion
  • Lines and mooring condition
  • Wear, damage and corrosion

  • Interior surfaces and woodwork
  • Visible leaks and moisture
  • Mold and mildew
  • Visible wood deterioration or possible termite activity
  • Other visible changes requiring attention

  • Bilge condition
  • Bilge pumps
  • Float switches
  • Visible water-system issues
  • Authorized engine exercise/check when appropriate

What happens next

When we see something changing, we document it and let you know. When routine care is approaching, we remind you.

When work is needed, you decide whether you'd like us to handle it or, when specialized expertise is required, help connect you with the right marine professional.

Less for you to monitor. More continuity for your boat.
