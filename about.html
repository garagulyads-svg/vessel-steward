About Vessel Steward

Local, hands-on boat care built around one idea: someone responsible should be watching your boat, so you don't have to.

Vessel Steward works directly on vessels on O‘ahu — checking condition, tracking what needs attention and carrying out or coordinating the maintenance and restoration that follows.

Every check and every piece of work gets recorded, so the vessel's history doesn't depend on any one person's memory — owner's or Vessel Steward's.
