Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Date — add before launch]

Vessel Steward respects your privacy and collects only the information reasonably necessary to communicate with you and provide our services.

Information We May Collect

When you contact Vessel Steward, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address, vessel information, marina or general vessel location, photographs, and information you voluntarily provide about your vessel or requested services.

For Vessel Stewardship clients, we may also maintain photographs, inspection notes, maintenance records, service history, and other information related to the care of the vessel.

How We Use Information

We may use this information to:

  • respond to inquiries;
  • evaluate requested work;
  • schedule and provide services;
  • communicate about the condition and maintenance of a vessel;
  • maintain service and Boat Care Journal records;
  • coordinate approved work with third-party marine professionals;
  • maintain business and service records.

We do not sell personal information.

Service Providers

When necessary to provide or coordinate an authorized service, relevant information may be shared with service providers or marine professionals involved in that work.

Data and Boat Care Records

Boat Care Journal information may include photographs, observations, maintenance history and records relating to a vessel.

Features, storage practices and access options for the Boat Care Journal may change as the service develops.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no electronic storage or communication system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
