Information on this website describes the general services offered by Vessel Steward. Actual services, scope of work, pricing and scheduling depend on the vessel, its condition, location and the work requested.

Vessel Stewardship Checks

Routine Vessel Stewardship visits are visual, non-invasive condition checks of reasonably accessible areas. They are intended to help identify visible changes or conditions that may require attention. They are not marine surveys and do not replace inspections or diagnoses performed by licensed, certified or otherwise qualified specialists.

A routine Stewardship visit cannot guarantee detection of every defect, leak, mechanical problem, electrical issue, structural condition, pest infestation or other hidden problem.

Hidden Conditions

Some damage or deterioration may exist inside wood, cabinetry, walls, equipment, machinery or other inaccessible areas without visible exterior signs. Vessel Steward is not responsible for conditions that could not reasonably be identified through the agreed scope of a visual, non-invasive check.

Termites and Pest Activity

When accessible wood surfaces are reviewed, Vessel Steward may note visible signs that could indicate deterioration or possible pest activity. These observations do not constitute a professional termite or pest inspection and do not guarantee that a vessel is free from termites or other pests.

Professional diagnosis or treatment should be performed by an appropriately qualified pest-control provider when required.

Engines and Vessel Systems

Engine exercise, pump checks and other basic system checks are performed only when included in the agreed service and when reasonably appropriate. These checks do not constitute comprehensive mechanical, electrical or systems inspections and do not guarantee future operation or reliability.

Specialized Services

Certain work may require specialized marine contractors, technicians or other professionals. When requested, Vessel Steward may assist with identifying or coordinating third-party providers. Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, third-party providers remain responsible for their own work, recommendations, warranties and services.

Estimates and Restoration Work

Restoration and repair estimates may change if concealed damage, deterioration or other conditions become apparent after work begins. Any material change in scope will be discussed with the vessel owner before additional work proceeds whenever reasonably possible.

Maintenance Recommendations

Maintenance schedules and reminders are provided as an organizational and preventive-care tool. Actual maintenance requirements may vary based on vessel use, equipment, manufacturer recommendations, environmental conditions and changes in vessel condition.

The vessel owner remains responsible for approving maintenance decisions and informing Vessel Steward of relevant changes to the vessel, its equipment or use.
